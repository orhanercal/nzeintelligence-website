Know the year your building reaches net zero.

Most net zero claims ignore the energy it takes to make a building. NZE Intelligence balances embodied energy, operational energy and on-site renewables, and tells you, at the design stage, when the energy debt is repaid.

From the first design model to a net zero year

  1. Measure what it takes to build

    Upload a Revit material take-off. Embodied energy is calculated for every material from established cradle-to-gate factors.

  2. Add how it will run

    Heating, cooling, lighting and appliances are set against the solar and wind energy the building can generate on site.

  3. See the year and what to change

    Get the year the building reaches realistic net zero, plus lower-energy material, design and renewable options that bring it forward.

Built on peer-reviewed research

NZE Intelligence turns the Realistic Net Zero Energy Building (RNZEB) method into a design tool. The method was developed in a Master’s dissertation awarded the Dean’s Prize for Innovation and Impact at Brunel University London.

  • Rethinking net zero: a new perspective on achieving net zero energy buildingsErcal, O. and Shafique, M. Sustainable Horizons, 2026
  • From design to decarbonisation: a BIM-based comparative analysis of embodied carbon in buildingsErcal, O. and Shafique, M. Carbon Footprints, 2026

Founder

Orhan Ercal is an architect and sustainability researcher with ten years of design and construction experience in the UK and Türkiye. He founded NZE Intelligence to give architects and developers a clear, early answer to the question that matters: when does this building actually reach net zero?
